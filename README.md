# Alexandria

Named after the ancient Library of Alexandria, Alexandria is an offline e-book library manager designed for Kobo e-readers.

Set out to enable offline use of your devices, it works by acting as a transparent proxy between your Kobo and the Kobo store, intercepting sync endpoints to seamlessly inject your personal library into the standard Kobo reading experience.

Your books appear alongside store purchases on your device, complete with covers, reading progress, and metadata — no sideloading or USB transfers required. Simply point your Kobo at Alexandria, drop EPUB files into the library folder, and your books will sync automatically.
